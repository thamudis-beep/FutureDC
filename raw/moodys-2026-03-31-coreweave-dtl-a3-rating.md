# Moody's Ratings assigns A3 to CoreWeave Compute Acquisition Co. VIII, LLC

**Toronto, March 31, 2026** — Moody's Ratings assigns a first time **A3** rating to CoreWeave Compute Acquisition Co. VIII, LLC's (the Borrower or Project Co) Senior Secured Delayed Draw Term Loan (DDTL) facility of approximately **$8.5 billion**. The rating outlook is **stable**.

## Facility structure

The DDTL includes:
- ~$4,041M fixed rate commitments
- ~$4,459M floating rate commitments (floating tranche at least 95% hedged through maturity)

The loans **fully amortize** and mature less than five years after the end of the **15-month delayed draw availability period**.

Loan proceeds will finance GPU infrastructure supporting **CoreWeave, Inc.'s (Ba3 stable)** obligations under a **six-year Master Services Agreement (MSA) with Meta Platforms, Inc. (Aa3 stable)**. Per the MSA, which is assigned to the Borrower, CoreWeave will supply **NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 servers** deployed across three sites in **Virginia, North Dakota, and Georgia** – the Project.

## Ratings Rationale

The A3 rating reflects the Borrower's **highly predictable revenue profile**, underpinned by a long-term, fixed-payment **take-or-pay MSA** with Meta. The fixed payments are independent of usage and the MSA cannot be terminated for convenience. The rating incorporates the current **supply constraints for available high-performance compute capacity**.

Robust structural protections support the A3 rating:
- Strict funding and commissioning requirements ensuring lenders finance only after completion of Level 3 testing
- Comprehensive direct agreement with Meta granting lenders cure, assignment, and replacement contract rights
- License of CoreWeave's operational IP, ensuring uninterrupted operations even if CoreWeave enters bankruptcy

While Meta can terminate the MSA under force majeure, Moody's finds this unlikely given the three project locations.

## Execution and operating risks

The Project's execution and operating risks are well mitigated by:
- CoreWeave's proven deployment and operating capabilities
- **Dell Inc.'s (Baa3 stable)** turnkey integration and delivery role
- Strict commissioning conditions

**CoreWeave's historical service level agreement breaches under similar MSAs have been very low at below 1%**, apply only to affected racks, and exclude outages caused by scheduled maintenance, power failures, hardware failures, force majeure, or customer-side issues.

CoreWeave's operating history in AI-focused GPU-as-a-service for a large hyperscaler is relatively short (**about three years**); however, this is partially mitigated by demonstrated execution across multiple large-scale GPU deployments and lender step-in rights.

## Power cost risk

Power cost risks must be well managed and hedged because top-line payments from Meta are fixed, so all operating costs (including power, data center, and capital) must be covered under these fixed payments.

Project Co has incorporated extensive power-price mitigation strategies:
- Largest site benefits from a long-dated fixed-for-floating hedge covering a substantial portion of power costs
- Dynamic power reserve mechanism traps cash as unhedged power costs approach conservatively modeled thresholds
- Three-month liquidity reserve covers debt service, operating expenses, and hedge settlements

## Debt service coverage

- Borrower's base case: **DSCR averages 1.26x through 2031**
- Moody's base case: DSCR averages **1.20x** through 2031
- Severe stressed scenarios: minimum DSCRs **above 1.11x**

The fully amortizing debt structure within the term of the MSA **eliminates refinancing and recontracting risk**.

## Rating sensitivity

**Upgrade triggers:**
- All compute infrastructure successfully installed, satisfying MSA requirements
- Track record of strong operating performance above expectations
- Moody's DSCRs exceed base case expectations on sustained basis

**Downgrade triggers:**
- Failure to install GPU infrastructure on contractual timelines, resulting in pricing penalties, loss of tranche revenues, or termination rights
- Operational disruption or unexpected higher power costs result in materially weaker DSCRs
- Material deterioration in Meta's credit quality
- Event of force majeure

## Structure

CoreWeave Compute Acquisition Co. VIII, LLC is a Delaware-organized, **bankruptcy-remote special purpose vehicle** established exclusively to finance, own, and operate the GPU infrastructure underpinning the Project. The Borrower sits within a wholly-owned, multi-tiered corporate chain with CoreWeave, Inc. as the ultimate parent. The ownership stack provides clear collateral pathways through pledged equity at each intermediate holding level, with strong bankruptcy-remoteness and separateness protections that ensure Project Co remains **ring-fenced** from any deterioration in its single-owner's credit quality.
