# Meta Platforms, Inc.

US hyperscaler. Runs a large first-party AI compute footprint and is
increasingly an **offtaker of Neocloud-built GPU capacity**.

## Credit profile

- **Aa3 stable** (Moody's) — high investment grade. [^moodys-2026-03]

## Active compute procurement

| Counterparty | Structure | Assets | Tenor | Size | Sites |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [CoreWeave](coreweave.md) | 6yr take-or-pay MSA | NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 | 6yr | backs $8.5B SPV | VA, ND, GA[^moodys-2026-03] |

- Payments to SPV are **fixed, independent of usage, non-terminable for
  convenience**; can terminate only under force majeure. [^moodys-2026-03]

## Relevance

- Meta's AAA-adjacent balance sheet is the credit-uplift engine for
  Neocloud project finance — demonstrates how [hyperscaler
  offtakes](../concepts/hyperscaler-offtake.md) work in practice.
