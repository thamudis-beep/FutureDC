# CoreWeave, Inc.

Publicly-listed **Neocloud** — GPU-cloud specialist renting NVIDIA AI
infrastructure at scale to hyperscalers and AI labs.

## Credit profile

- **Parent rated Ba3 stable** by Moody's (speculative grade). [^moodys-2026-03]
- Individual project-finance SPVs can achieve much higher ratings — e.g.
  **A3 stable** for Compute Acquisition Co. VIII via Meta backstop —
  approximately **5-notch uplift** from ring-fenced SPV + IG counterparty. [^moodys-2026-03]

## Operating profile

- ~3 years operating history in AI-focused GPU-as-a-service for large
  hyperscalers. [^moodys-2026-03]
- **Historical SLA breaches <1%** under large hyperscaler MSAs
  (excluding scheduled maintenance, power failures, hardware failures,
  force majeure, customer-side). [^moodys-2026-03]
