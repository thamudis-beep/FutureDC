# Moody's Ratings

One of the "Big Three" NRSRO credit rating agencies (alongside S&P, Fitch).
Wholly-owned subsidiary of Moody's Corporation (MCO).

## Relevance to AI Economics

- Rates **project-finance SPVs** funding AI GPU clusters — ratings signal
  how institutional credit markets assess GPU cluster durability, useful
  life, and counterparty risk.
- Has published rating actions on CoreWeave and CoreWeave-sponsored SPVs;
  rated CoreWeave parent **Ba3 stable** and at least one GPU cluster SPV
  (Compute Acquisition Co. VIII) **A3 stable**. [^moodys-2026-03]

## Recent actions

- **2026-03-31**: First-time A3 (stable) on CoreWeave Compute Acquisition
  Co. VIII, LLC — $8.5B Senior Secured DDTL financing GB300 NVL72 servers
  under a 6-year take-or-pay MSA with Meta. [^moodys-2026-03]

## Methodology

- Primary methodology used for CoreWeave SPV: **Generic Project Finance**
  (published October 2024). [^moodys-2026-03]
